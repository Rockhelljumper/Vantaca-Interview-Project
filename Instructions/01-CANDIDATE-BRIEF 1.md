# Integration Lead Exercise — Northwind Bank

## The situation

We're building an integration with **Northwind Bank** so our product can:

- Show customers their linked account balances and recent transactions
- Let customers initiate ACH transfers between accounts

Northwind is a large, important partner. Their integration team has sent us their
API documentation (`02-NORTHWIND-CONNECT-API.md`) and there's an email/Slack
thread capturing what they — and our own product owner — are asking us to build
(`03-INTEGRATION-THREAD.md`).

**Read both documents before the session.**

## What we're asking you to do

You're joining as the **lead** on this integration. Review the API docs and the
thread, then come ready to walk us through how you'd deliver it. There's no
"gotcha" algorithm and no finished implementation required — we want to see how
you think about the work and the decisions you'd make.

Come prepared to talk through:

1. **Your plan.** How would you build this integration? Sketch the architecture
   however you like — whiteboard, diagram, or a few paragraphs — and walk us
   through it.

2. **How you'd deliver it.** Where would you start, what would you build first,
   and how would you get to something you'd be confident shipping to customers?

3. **Your questions.** What would you want to clarify with Northwind or with our
   product owner before committing to a direction?

**You can ask us those questions live.** During the session we'll stand in for
both Northwind's integration team and our product owner. If there's something
you'd want to know from them — about their API, their constraints, our
priorities, anything — just ask, and we'll answer in role. You don't have to
design in a vacuum or guess at the unknowns.

## Optional (bring it if you'd like)

If you want to show code, sketch or implement the function that submits a
transfer to Northwind. Use any language and any tooling you normally reach for,
including LLM tooling — we're interested in *how* you work, not in whether you
memorized an SDK.

## How we'll spend the time

- ~10 min: you walk us through your plan at a high level
- ~30–40 min: we dig into your plan, your questions, and the decisions behind
  them together (expect us to play the role of Northwind and of our product
  owner)
- ~10 min: your questions for us

There are no trick answers. The best sessions feel like a design review with a
peer, not an exam.
